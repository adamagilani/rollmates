RollMates Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

RollMates develops your photos like film and lets you share rolls with friends ("mates"). This policy explains what information RollMates uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: your photos are processed on your iPhone, you choose what to share, and we don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Your photos

  • Developing happens on your iPhone. Photos you import are processed on your device. Your full-quality photos stay on your iPhone and are not uploaded to us, unless you subscribe to RollMates Cloud (below).
  • Sharing a roll uploads smaller copies of the photos you choose (up to 2048 pixels), with camera details (like camera model and settings), the date each photo was taken, the roll's name, film, note, and place name if you added one. Shared copies are made fresh from the image, without GPS coordinates or other hidden metadata. Only your mates can see a shared roll, and you can unshare it at any time, which deletes the copies from our servers.

Your account

Signing in is optional; you can develop rolls without an account. If you create one, we store:

  • your email address (to sign you in; it is never shown to other people),
  • your name, username and profile photo, which your mates and people you invite can see,
  • your mates, mate requests, and anyone you've blocked,
  • reactions and comments you leave on mates' rolls, which the roll's owner and their mates can see.

Films you publish

If you publish a film you made, its name, description, tags, settings and your username become public so anyone using RollMates can find, save and remix it. You can unpublish it at any time. Films you keep private are backed up to your account so you don't lose them, and are visible only to you.

Location

RollMates only uses your location if you choose to tag a roll with where it was taken. The place is saved with the roll on your iPhone. If you share the roll, only the place name (like "San Francisco, CA") is shared, never your exact coordinates. You can turn off location access at any time in the Settings app.

Apple Intelligence

Any features that use Apple Intelligence run on Apple's on-device model. What you type and the photos it looks at are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications and sign in, we store your iPhone's notification address with your account so we can tell you about mate requests, new rolls from your mates, and reactions, comments and mentions on rolls. Notifications are delivered by Apple. Signing out removes this iPhone's address from your account, and you can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.

Reports and safety

If you report a person, a roll or a film, your report (and your username) goes to the RollMates team. To check a report, the team can view the reported roll. We may hide rolls, remove films or close accounts that break our Terms of Use.

Feedback

When you send feedback in the app, we receive your message and, if you choose, a screenshot and an email address to reply to. To help fix problems, feedback also includes the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, the screen you were on, the last error message the app showed, a random identifier for your installation, and your username if you're signed in. Only the RollMates team can read feedback.

Crash reports

If RollMates crashes or freezes, iOS gives the app a technical report the next time you open it (Apple's MetricKit), and the app sends it to us so we can fix the problem. A report says what went wrong and where in RollMates' code, with the app version, iOS version and iPhone model. It doesn't include your photos, your account or your username; it's tied only to a random identifier for your installation. Only the RollMates team can read crash reports.

RollMates Cloud

RollMates Cloud is an optional subscription (Cloud Pro or Cloud Max) that backs up your photos so you can restore them on any iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. We keep a record of your subscription (which plan, when it renews or ends) with your account, and Apple tells our server about renewals, cancellations and refunds.

  • What's backed up: your photos at full quality (each photo's original and its finished version), small thumbnails, and their details: file name, camera details and the date taken, your edits, favorites, and your rolls' names, notes, film and place. If you turn on Keep RAW files, your camera's RAW files are backed up too and then removed from your iPhone to save space.
  • Who can see it: only you. Your backup is never shared with your mates or anyone else.
  • Where it's stored: the files are stored, encrypted at rest, with our storage provider Backblaze (in the United States); the details are stored with Supabase. Your iPhone uploads and downloads files through short-lived links our server creates only for your own files.
  • Deleting: photos you delete from your Library stay in Cloud's Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they're removed for good. You can remove any roll from Cloud yourself at any time.
  • If your subscription ends: your backup stays available to view and download for 60 days, then it's deleted. The app reminds you before that happens.

How we store and protect information

Account data, shared rolls, published films, feedback and crash reports are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase (RollMates Cloud backups are stored with Backblaze, as described above), and protected so that people can only see what they're allowed to (for example, only your mates can see your shared rolls). Notifications are delivered by Apple. We don't use advertising or third-party analytics services, and we don't sell or rent your information.

Deleting your information

You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, mates, shared rolls and their photos, your films and your RollMates Cloud backup from our servers. Rolls on your iPhone are not affected; delete the app to remove them.

Children

RollMates is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for significant changes, let you know in the app.
