01 · Store

Every shoot, kept as a roll.

Plug your SD card into your iPhone and your new photos are waiting. Each shoot becomes a roll, with its date and place.

  • RAW or JPEG, straight off the card. No computer needed.
  • Only new shots show up, so nothing gets imported twice.
  • A full-quality master of every photo is kept, so you can re-edit anytime.

03 · Share

Share the roll, not just one photo.

RollMates is for your mates, not a feed for strangers. Share a whole roll, or pick who sees it, and see theirs.

  • Share with all your mates, or just the ones you choose.
  • Reactions and comments on every roll.
  • Turn a few frames into an Instagram Story in one tap.
  • Invite friends with a link or a QR code.
